# Paper trading workflow

1. Record a simulation only after event, selection, price and stake are explicit.
2. Do not use this workflow to place or imply a real wager.
3. Settle only against a confirmed result and retain the outcome.
4. Review ROI, win rate, net profit, open exposure and drawdown together.
5. Do not judge a strategy from a small sample; include sample size and period.
6. Export the local dashboard when a human-readable report is requested.
